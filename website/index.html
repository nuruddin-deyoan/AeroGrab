Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A focused tool that does one job well — getting videos off YouTube and onto your disk.

Singles, playlists & channels

Paste one video, a full playlist, a channel, or a batch of links. Select all, pick a range, or filter by title.

Best → 360p + MP3

Six quality presets from original Best down to 360p, plus one-click MP3 audio extraction. Set a default once.

Smart download queue

Up to 5 concurrent downloads with live speed and ETA, per-item cancel, one-click retry for failures, and reveal-in-folder.

History that works for you

Every finished download is logged with size and date, grouped by day, searchable — and re-downloadable in one click.

Truly offline-first

yt-dlp and ffmpeg ship inside the installer. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry — your links never leave your PC.

Yours to tune

Default quality, download folder with free-space readout, concurrency control — every change saves instantly.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is AeroGrab really free?
Yes — free forever, no accounts, no ads, no feature gates. If it ever asks for payment, it isn't us.
Does it work offline?
The app itself is fully offline-first: yt-dlp and ffmpeg ship inside the installer. You only need internet for the obvious part — fetching video info and downloading.
Why is the installer ~190 MB?
Almost all of it is the bundled ffmpeg.exe (~146 MB) for merging and MP3 conversion, plus the Electron runtime. That's the price of zero extra installs.
Windows SmartScreen warns me. Is it safe?
The build isn't code-signed (certificates cost money), so SmartScreen flags it. The source is open — click More info → Run anyway, or build from source yourself.
What can I download, and in which quality?
Single videos, playlists, channels, and bulk URL lists — in Best, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, or MP3 audio-only.
Which Windows versions are supported?
Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. You'll need roughly 500 MB of free disk space for the app plus room for your videos.
How do I get updates?
There's no auto-updater yet — watch the releases page; new versions land there with a changelog.
